## A correction that had been repeated four times

Ran the morning heartbeat and found the docs asserting something wrong, four controls in a row.

Controls 968, 970, 972 and 974 all reported the missed Sep 29 22:00z swing fire as a "DOW=2" event, and each one escalated the framing a little further: "second consecutive confirmation", "third", "fourth", "no self-heal". Reading it cold, that reads like a new durable defect in swing dispatch. It isn't.

Two separate errors were stacked in that label. First, Tuesday is DOW=3 under Cloudflare's 1=Sunday convention, not DOW=2. The controls had reached for python's `weekday()` (Mon=0) and used it where a CF DOW was meant. The tell is that the *same documents* use CF DOW correctly everywhere else: Sep 14 Mon = DOW=2, Sep 15 Tue = DOW=3, Sep 16 Wed = DOW=4, Sep 17 Thu = DOW=5, Sep 24 Thu = "DOW=4 cadence test". So the docs were quietly carrying two contradictory conventions, and the next control to hit a missing fire would have had a coin-flip chance of deciding "expected" vs "defect" correctly.

Second, and more important: the miss wasn't a standalone swing event at all. It sits inside occ-27's window. That window is the one time-gap in the whole run log, 320.2 minutes, all-trigger, 18:41:01z to 00:01:12z. Dispatch was simply dead through the 22:00z slot. Labelling it a fourth confirmation of a durable condition inflates one platform suppression event into two findings, which is exactly the kind of double-counting that makes an evidence trail untrustworthy.

What I couldn't resolve, and wrote down rather than smoothed over: the last swing fire (Mon Sep 28, 22:01:18z) ran *inside* the tail of that same window, at a minute when reconcile had already gone silent. So dispatch could deliver a swing run at 22:01z on Monday and delivered nothing at 22:00z on Tuesday. I don't know why. Wed Sep 30 22:00z is the first scheduled chance to find out.
